01 // about

Who I Am

My journey into embedded systems began in 2022, during my engineering preparatory studies, when the first generative AI models transformed the way we interact with technology. While many were fascinated by AI itself, I became curious about the systems that make it possible. I realized that every intelligent application ultimately depends on reliable hardware, and that's what led me to specialize in embedded systems—a choice I'm proud of every day.

Today, I'm Abdellah Taouil, a 5th-year engineering student pursuing a double degree in Computer Science and Embedded Electronic Systems at Polytech Grenoble INP, University of Grenoble Alpes, after completing two years of engineering studies in Embedded Systems and Digital Services at the National Institute of Posts and Telecommunications (INPT) in Rabat.

I enjoy designing complete systems from idea to deployment, combining digital design, embedded firmware, AI, IoT, and modern software development. Whether developing FPGA architectures, programming STM32 microcontrollers, integrating Raspberry Pi-based vision systems, or building mobile and web applications that interact with embedded devices, I enjoy bridging hardware and software to create practical, production-ready solutions.

Along the way, I've strengthened these skills through internships and research experiences in Belgium and France, leading the Robotics Cell at CESE INPT, participating in robotics competitions and hackathons and continuously learning through personal projects and professional certifications. Every experience has reinforced the same goal: transforming innovative ideas into reliable technologies that solve real-world problems.

04 // experience

Work Experience

NFC-based Firmware Update for STM32 Devices
Multitel — Innovation Centre (Internship)
Apr. 2026 — Sept. 2026
Mons, Belgium
  • Designed and implemented an end-to-end firmware update solution over NFC for STM32-based embedded systems. The solution combines a custom STM32 bootloader, an ST25DV NFC interface, a Flutter Android application, and a FastAPI backend to enable reliable local firmware updates without physical access to the device.
  • Developed the STM32 bootloader and OTA update protocol, including firmware metadata management, chunk-based data transfer, Flash memory programming, update state management, error handling, and CRC32-based integrity verification. The protocol was designed around the constraints of the ST25DV Fast Transfer Mode and the limited memory resources of the microcontroller.
  • Developed a Flutter-based Android maintenance application to automate the complete update workflow: NFC device detection and identification, firmware version retrieval, firmware download, data fragmentation, sequential NFC transmission, bootloader response handling, progress monitoring, and final update status reporting.
  • Developed a FastAPI backend and database for centralized firmware management, including firmware storage, version control, metadata management, CRC information, and device/project-specific firmware distribution to the mobile application.
  • Designed and integrated the complete system architecture spanning the embedded firmware, NFC communication layer, Android application, and backend. The solution was tested and validated on the target STM32/ST25DV hardware platform to ensure correct end-to-end firmware transfer and installation.
  • Integrated cybersecurity considerations inspired by the EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA), with particular attention to firmware integrity and secure update mechanisms. The architecture was designed with further security measures in mind, including firmware authentication, anti-rollback protection, secure boot, and firmware encryption.
