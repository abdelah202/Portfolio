01 // about

Who I Am

I'm Abdellah Taouil, a 4th-year engineering student pursuing a double degree in Computer Science and Embedded Electronic Systems at Polytech Grenoble INP, University of Grenoble Alpes — after completing two years of engineering studies in Embedded Systems and Digital Services at the National Institute of Posts and Telecommunications in Rabat.

My passion sits at the intersection of hardware and software. I'm equally comfortable writing VHDL for an FPGA timing-critical block and building a production-grade Android application in Flutter that communicates with it. That dual fluency is what drives me.

I've had the chance to apply these skills in real environments: designing an NFC-based OTA firmware update system for STM32 devices at Multitel (Belgium), implementing a digital PLL for IoT backscattering at TIMA Laboratory (Grenoble), and working on AI-based battery estimation for electric vehicles at Segula Technologies.

Outside of engineering, I've led a robotics club, competed in robotics competitions, and enjoy staying curious through travel and fitness. I'm trilingual — Arabic, French, and English — which helps me collaborate in international teams and adapt quickly to new environments.
